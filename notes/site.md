# Notes for the future exo.askaconsult.com/os page

Running list. Do not build the page yet: A wants it written after the build exists, so it shows what the
OS really looks like (BRIEF-07). Everything here is an idea to capture once it is real. Same tone as the Exo
site: plain, warm, short sentences, jargon explained.

## One-line ideas (candidate headlines)

- Your machine is a view of you.
- One line to type into. Everything else appears when it helps.
- Every change has an undo, and the undo says what it does in plain words.
- It asks rarely, and only when it matters.
- The same Exo on your desk, in your voice and on your phone.
- Your computer forgets nothing, and you can read why it did what it did.
- Built on normal Linux and normal apps. Nothing to give up.
